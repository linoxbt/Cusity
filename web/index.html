The bot filter
you can check.

Cusity is one learned logic circuit, taped out on X Layer. No weights file, no server. Every entrant can see the bits it read, why it decided, and how accurate it is on chain.

Check a wallet
    • Any X Layer wallet becomes 64 bits at a fixed snapshot block.
    • Open-source extractor, hash pinned on chain.
    • Every entrant's bits fixed by a Merkle root.
    • The circuit's netlist hash goes on chain before the snapshot.
    • Bound after tape-out only if the hashes match.
    • The filter can't change after the fact.
    • A difflogic network compiled to NAND gates.
    • Evaluated by the TapeOut processor with eval.
    • Your browser re-runs every gate to confirm.
    • A future block hash picks test rows.
    • The contract scores the circuit on chain.
    • Abandoned rounds stay on record as expired.

Overview

Cusity is a bot filter for airdrops and campaigns that is committed before the snapshot, checkable by every entrant, and audited on chain. A project commits the circuit, entrants check themselves, and disputes become a lookup instead of an argument.
